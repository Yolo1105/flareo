Aladdin Artists, creates and orchestrates a vast array of music, art, dance, film, television and theatrical works. And activates entertainment experiences across multiple platforms.
Executive Producer, Richard L Gant earned his credentials early in life while working as a professional musician (trumpet) and composer in various artistic settings including live performance, composition, theatrical music direction and production. As Director of Special Events at the world famous Palladium in New York City, he lead the creative for the production and technical teams on multiple legendary events Including, Opening Announcement of Universal Orlando, hosted by Steven Spielberg and Ron Howard.
Richard received music conservatory training at Howard University under the direction of Trumpeter, Donald Byrd and continues to play professionally as time permits.
