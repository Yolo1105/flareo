# Contributing

This project is **intentionally frozen** at a high-quality snapshot.
It is complete for its purpose as an engineering credibility artifact.

- **Feature requests** are out of scope. See [`KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
- **Security issues** go through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Yolo1105/flareo/security/advisories/new).
- **Bug reports** are welcome and may or may not be acted on.
- There is **no roadmap** and **no release cadence**.
- License is **Apache-2.0** — fork freely.
